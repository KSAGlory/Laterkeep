# KSA WindowSnooze support

## Before opening an issue

Confirm that you are using the latest official release and that the target is a normal visible desktop window. A normally launched application may not be able to manage windows running with administrator privileges or protected system windows.

## Product issues

Open a [GitHub issue](https://github.com/KSAGlory/KSA-WindowSnooze/issues) and include:

- the WindowSnooze version;
- your Windows version;
- the application whose window you tried to snooze;
- clear reproduction steps;
- what you expected and what happened instead.

Do not include passwords, tokens, private documents, confidential window titles, or other sensitive information.
